# Meister 6.43

`meister purge` now shows what it is doing and explains what Finder will show afterwards.

- Live progress. While checking or evicting iCloud files, purge prints the item count and GB so far: once at the start, about every 10 seconds, and at the end. A run with thousands of files can take an hour, and before this it gave no output during that time. Progress appears when the output is a terminal. Set `PURGE_PROGRESS=1` or `PURGE_PROGRESS=0` to force it on or off.
- Explanation in Finder's terms. Finder's "Available" ("Verfügbar") already includes purgeable space, so evicting files does not raise it; the space moves from purgeable to free. The preview says so before you run anything, and `--do` repeats it after the measured result, with a hint to reopen Get Info (Cmd+I).

Validation: 4 new Bats tests, including one against the real JXA helper. The full quality gate passed. A real preview on this Mac reported progress for 12,240 items in 17 seconds.

Recap: purge now reports progress during long runs and explains that Finder's "Available" stays the same while purgeable space turns into free space.
